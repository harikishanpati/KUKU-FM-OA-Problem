He is not answering my calls now.

1. STRUCTURED CASE SUMMARY:
   Domain:          Tenant Security Deposit Disputes
   Amount Claimed:  ₹65,000
   Existing Proof:  WhatsApp chat logs / agreement confirmations
   Missing Proof:   Signed Rental Agreement / Lease Deed, Proof of security deposit payment (bank transaction receipt/UPI/cheque), Move-in and move-out inspection photos/videos or handover email confirmation, Written communication (WhatsApp/Email) showing notice given and demand for refund

2. LEGAL STANDING & ACTIONABLE OPTIONS:
   Standing:        Strong Prima Facie Case under Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (Section 108) & State Rent Control Acts / Model Tenancy Act
   Explanation:     Under Indian law, the other party cannot arbitrarily withhold your money without documented justification. You have a strong legal right to seek full refund of ₹65,000.
   • Step 1: Send a Formal Written Demand Notice giving 15 clear days to settle.
   • Step 2: File a free pre-litigation grievance on government portals (e.g., National Consumer Helpline 1915 or State Labour Portal).
   • Step 3: If unresponsive, escalate to the appropriate statutory forum (District Consumer Commission via e-Daakhil or Rent Authority).

   Opposing Landlord: 'Look at clause 8 of the agreement, painting charges are always deducted from deposit. Everybody in Bangalore does this. Take ₹25,000 or wait 2 months.'
